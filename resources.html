Resources

Models and data used to build AuroraChem

Models

Aurora
Atmospheric foundation model from Microsoft. AuroraChem is fine-tuned from its pretrained weights.
GEOS-Chem
Global chemical transport model. Source of the chemical training fields.
FETCH4
Fate, Emissions, and Transport of CH4 project. Provided the GEOS-Chem simulation with 14CO.

Data

ERA5
ECMWF reanalysis. Source of the meteorological training fields.
CAMS global emissions
Monthly anthropogenic emissions of CO, NOx, NH3, and SO2.
TROPOMI NO2
Satellite NO2 retrievals from Sentinel-5P.

Related models

gSAM-Chem
